Netherlands

Home of ASML, the only maker of the machines that print the most advanced chips. It joined Washington's chip club in June 2026 while fighting Washington's attempt to write its export rules.

Stated position

The Hague insists it decides its own export policy. In 2026 its trade minister flew to Washington to lobby against the MATCH Act, a US bill that would push allies to match American chip controls on China.

Both governments agree on keeping ASML's most advanced EUV machines out of China. They disagree on how far to go with older equipment.

Alignment

Despite that dispute, the foreign ministry announced on 23 June 2026 that it would join Pax Silica. It also signed the Finland–Norway call for control of frontier models and the Paris Call.

Chips

ASML is the textbook chokepoint. But research based on interviews with former officials suggests it gave the Netherlands little leverage on its own in export-control talks with Washington.

Laws and rules

As an EU member, it falls under the EU AI Act. We have not found a dedicated national AI safety institute.
