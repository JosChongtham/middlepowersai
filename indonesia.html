Indonesia

Southeast Asia's largest economy joined Beijing's AI organisation as a founder, while its own AI rules are still waiting for the president's signature.

Stated position

Jakarta wants to shape AI governance rather than only consume foreign technology. In ASEAN it promotes AI that is sovereign, interoperable and trusted, and it has urged WAICO to work alongside the UN rather than duplicate it.

Laws and rules

Two presidential regulations, one setting a national AI roadmap for 2026 to 2029 and one on AI ethics, were still pending in mid-2026 after long consultations. Officials say they will be human-centred and risk-based.

Money and compute

The draft roadmap plans a sovereign AI fund run mainly by Danantara, the new national wealth fund, and would build AI into flagship programmes such as free school meals.

Alignment

One of 29 founding members of the World AI Cooperation Organization. We have not found a dedicated government AI safety institute.

  • How Jakarta balances WAICO with its ASEAN and Western ties.
