Canada

The country trying hardest to turn middle-power talk into a real coalition, with a dedicated AI minister and a $2 billion sovereign compute plan.

Stated position

Under Mark Carney, Canada has been the loudest advocate of middle powers acting together. Evan Solomon became its first minister for AI, and the AI for All strategy launched on 4 June 2026.

Solomon has said the previous government over-indexed on regulation. Critics such as Michael Geist say the strategy funds infrastructure but defers on the law.

Safety institute

The Canadian AI Safety Institute launched in November 2024 with $50 million over five years. The 2026 strategy adds money to expand its model evaluations.

Compute

The $2 billion compute strategy includes $705 million for a Canada-owned supercomputer. Canada's three national AI institutes, Mila, Vector and Amii, anchor its research talent, and Cohere is its leading model maker.

Washington has flagged Canada's sovereign cloud plans as a trade concern.

Alignment

Signed the call for control of frontier models and the Paris Call, renewed its security pact with the UK, launched the Alliance for the Future with the EU, and formed a technology alliance with Germany.
