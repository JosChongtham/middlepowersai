Singapore

Stated position

Singapore takes a pro-innovation, non-prescriptive approach and builds credibility through practical tools rather than laws. It has issued governance frameworks since 2019 and runs the AI Verify testing toolkit.

Safety institute

The Digital Trust Centre at Nanyang Technological University was designated Singapore's AI Safety Institute on 22 May 2024. It runs joint tests with the Japanese, Canadian and Korean institutes and leads ASEAN's working group on AI governance.

Laws and rules

No AI-specific law. Voluntary frameworks cover traditional AI (2019), generative AI (2024) and, launched at Davos in January 2026, autonomous AI agents.

Alignment

A founding member of Pax Silica, and an endorser of the call for control of frontier models.
