About

Who writes this, and why

middlepowers.si is a one-person project that tracks how countries outside Washington and Beijing are trying to shape AI together.

I'm Jos Chongtham, a journalist and AI governance generalist based in Berlin.

Most coverage of AI politics is a two-player story: the United States against China. But a lot of the interesting moves are happening in between. Canada and Germany pool their compute, Finland and Norway draft a call for control of frontier models, India hosts a summit, and the Netherlands quietly holds one of the most important chokepoints in the whole supply chain. These middle powers can't win an AI race on their own. Together they might decide what the rules look like.

I started this site to follow that story closely and explain it plainly. The tracker records what was actually signed, by whom and when, with a source for every entry. The briefings try to say what it means, with a human touch rather than a wall of jargon.

I'd rather be useful than complete, and I'll get things wrong. If you spot a mistake, a missing signature or a development I should be following, please tell me.
