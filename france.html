France

Europe's loudest voice for AI sovereignty: host of two summits, home of Mistral, and an early signer of both middle-power safety texts.

Stated position

Paris hosted the AI Action Summit in February 2025 and convened the Paris Call in November 2026. Its consistent message is that Europe needs its own models and compute.

Safety institute

INESIA, the national institute for AI evaluation and security, was founded in February 2025 to coordinate French work on AI risk, regulation and testing. It adopted a roadmap for 2026 and 2027.

Compute and models

Mistral's compute platform runs 18,000 Nvidia Grace Blackwell chips in a 40-megawatt data centre in Essonne, and the company aims for a gigawatt of capacity in Europe by 2030. ASML is among its backers.

In January 2026 the armed forces ministry gave Mistral a framework agreement to supply AI across defence.

Laws and rules

The EU AI Act applies.
