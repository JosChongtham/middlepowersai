Taiwan

It makes most of the world's leading-edge chips but sits outside nearly every formal AI coalition. Its new AI law sets out principles, not obligations.

Stated position

Taipei talks of sovereign AI built on local data and democratic values. Its Ten AI Initiatives plan aims to make Taiwan a global AI leader by 2040.

AI also has a defence role: the defence ministry set up an AI office in late 2025.

Laws and rules

The AI Basic Act passed on 23 December 2025 and took effect on 14 January 2026. It sets seven principles and red lines, names the National Science and Technology Council as the lead authority, and asks the digital ministry to build a risk classification system.

It places no duties on companies yet, and critics say its enforcement power is weak. Government must bring other laws into line within two years.

Chips

TSMC makes the large majority of the world's most advanced chips. That gives Taipei a structural importance that no declaration captures.

Alignment

Its diplomatic position keeps it out of most formal coalitions, including those tracked on this site.
