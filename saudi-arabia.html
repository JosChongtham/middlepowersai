Saudi Arabia

The newest big spender: a state-backed AI company planning gigawatts of data centres, built almost entirely on American chips.

Stated position

Riyadh wants to become a leading AI power by 2030, as part of Vision 2030. HUMAIN, chaired by the crown prince, is the vehicle.

Compute

HUMAIN plans 3 gigawatts of data-centre capacity by 2030 and more than 6 by 2034. Nvidia's first tranche was 18,000 Blackwell chips, AMD signed a $10 billion deal, and an expanded Nvidia deal adds about 27,000 more GPUs.

Washington approved advanced chip exports to HUMAIN in November 2025.

Safety and rules

We have not found a dedicated government AI safety institute or an AI-specific law.

Alignment

Its partnerships are overwhelmingly with US companies, but it has not signed any of the agreements tracked on this site.

We have not confirmed a signature on any agreement tracked on this site.

What to watch
