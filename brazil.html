Stated position

Brasília speaks for the Global South on AI. It put AI governance on the agenda of its 2024 G20 presidency and runs a national AI plan for 2024 to 2028.

Laws and rules

Bill PL 2338/2023 would create a risk-based AI law. The Senate passed it on 10 December 2024. As of 5 November 2026 it was still waiting for a committee opinion in the Chamber of Deputies, with no vote scheduled.

Meanwhile the electoral court has issued rules on AI deepfakes in campaign advertising.

Alignment
