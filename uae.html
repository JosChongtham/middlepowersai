United Arab Emirates

It trades capital, cheap power and close ties to Washington for chips. Since July 2026 it can import advanced US AI chips without individual licences, the only Middle East state with that access.

Stated position

Abu Dhabi wants to be one of the world's main AI hubs. Its strategy runs through the US–UAE AI Acceleration Partnership signed in May 2025.

Compute

G42 is building Stargate UAE, a one-gigawatt cluster for OpenAI with Oracle, Cisco, Nvidia and SoftBank, inside a planned five-gigawatt campus. The first approved US chips arrived in May 2026.

In July 2026 Washington moved the UAE into the same export tier as Europe, South Korea and India. Some US lawmakers have opposed the chip deals.

Safety and rules

We have not found a dedicated government AI safety institute or an AI-specific law.

Alignment

Joined Pax Silica in January 2026 and endorsed the call for control of frontier models. It will host the AI summit after Geneva, in 2028.
