Stated position

Berlin frames AI as a question of digital sovereignty for its industrial base. Its High-Tech Agenda includes bringing one of the EU's AI gigafactories to Germany.

Compute

Deutsche Telekom and Nvidia opened the Industrial AI Cloud in Munich in February 2026, a privately funded project of about €1 billion that raises German AI computing power by around half.

It hosts SOOFI, a project to build open-source European foundation models.

Home-built models

Aleph Alpha is combining with Canada's Cohere, under a definitive agreement signed in September 2026. The company will operate as Cohere with bases in both countries, pending regulatory approval.

Laws and rules

The EU AI Act applies. We have not found a dedicated national AI safety institute.
