United Kingdom

Stated position

London sits closer to Washington than any other middle power: a founding Pax Silica signatory and a partner in US-led security work. It also wants to lead on AI safety diplomacy.

Chatham House argues it should use the 2027 summits to lead middle-power coordination and sign the middle powers' AI security statement.

Safety institute

The AI Safety Institute, renamed the AI Security Institute in 2025, published its first Frontier AI Trends Report in December 2025, drawing on two years of tests across more than thirty frontier systems.

It gets early access to new models from Google DeepMind, Anthropic and others, but only because the companies agree to it.

Laws and rules

There is no AI-specific law. The UK relies on existing laws and sector regulators. A bill to put the institute on a legal footing and let it compel testing has been promised repeatedly but not introduced.

In October 2026 the government proposed AI incident reporting through its Cyber Security and Resilience Bill. Separately, MP Alex Sobel has introduced a private member's bill to ban superintelligent AI.

Alignment

A founding member of Pax Silica in December 2025, and in October 2026 it renewed its AI security partnership with Canada.
